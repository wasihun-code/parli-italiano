## 1. Exact Lesson Generation Algorithm

The lesson generation is handled by `scripts/curriculum_designer.py` using a deterministic chunking strategy. 

### Pseudocode
```python
def chunk_list(data_list, n=6):
    chunk_size = len(data_list) // n
    remainder = len(data_list) % n
    chunks = []
    for i in range(n):
        # Distribute remainder items across the first few chunks
        size = chunk_size + (1 if i < remainder else 0)
```

## 2. Item Ordering (The "Alphabetical Trap")

### Vocabulary Ordering
Vocabulary is ordered **alphabetically**. 
- The `linguistic_extractor.py` performs a `sorted(list(all_words))` before assigning IDs `v1`, `v2`, etc.
- **Result:** Lesson 1 always contains words starting with 'A' and 'B', while Lesson 6 contains words starting with 'V' through 'Z'.

### Phrase Ordering
Phrases are ordered **alphabetically**.
- The extractor performs `sorted(list(all_phrases))` before assigning IDs `p1`, `p2`, etc.
- **Note:** While the UI components (like `PhraseTrainingScreen`) have a `sortPhrasesByDifficulty` function, the **Lesson Structure itself is fixed** to the alphabetical order generated by the factory.

### Sentence Ordering
Sentences are ordered **alphabetically**.
- The extractor performs `sorted(list(all_sentences))` before assigning IDs `s1`, `s2`, etc.
- Similar to phrases, the Factory ignores syntax or situational logic in favor of character-sort ordering.

## 3. Lesson Sizing Logic

Lesson sizes are determined purely by the total count divided by 6.
- If a scenario has 300 words, every lesson will have exactly 50 words.
- There is no "Mastery Buffer" or "Review Chunk"; every item is introduced exactly once in the mini-lessons.

## 4. Conversation Influence

**None.**
- The `curriculum_designer.py` does not analyze the conversation's turn-by-turn structure. 
- It treats the entire conversation corpus as a single "bag of words/phrases" and distributes them mathematically.
- A learner might learn a word in Lesson 1 that is only used in the final turn of the final conversation, or learn a word in Lesson 6 that is required for the very first message of the conversation.

## 5. Semantic & Difficulty Grouping

- **Semantic Grouping:** **NON-EXISTENT**. Words are not grouped by topic (e.g., "Food", "Numbers", "Directions") unless they happen to share the same starting letter.
- **Difficulty Grouping:** **NON-EXISTENT** at the Factory level. The Factory does not distinguish between a simple word like "ciao" and a complex one like "architettonico" when assigning lesson slots.

## 6. Audit Conclusion

The curriculum design is a **Mathematical Projection**, not a Pedagogical one. It ensures 100% coverage and determinism (the "Factory V2" goal), but it sacrifices the "narrative arc" of learning. The learner is essentially memorizing a dictionary slice before being allowed to speak.
